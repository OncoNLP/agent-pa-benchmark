## PSP Discovery — Breaking the F1 Ceiling

Our previous F1 ceiling of 0.572 across all conditions was entirely a data access
problem: SIGNOR + UniProt only covers ~48% recall against the gold standard. PSP is
the primary source in the gold standard (~15,600 human entries).

**Implementation:** Extended `_dispatch_http_get` in `qwen_prompt_testing/agent_with_http.py`
to detect the PSP URL by hostname, decompress the `.gz` response using Python's `gzip`
module, and parse the TSV into atlas entries — same accumulator pattern as SIGNOR.
Filters: `SUB_ORGANISM == "human"` only. Gene symbols uppercased to HGNC convention.

**Validation:** Local parse test confirmed 15,586 human entries from the 760KB file.
Smoke test (5 tool calls) confirmed Qwen follows the prompt: PSP (tool 1, 15,149
entries accumulated) → SIGNOR (tool 2, 9,669 new entries, 24,818 total).

**Multi-DB fix:** Also fixed `_accumulate_http_entries` to merge `supporting_databases`
lists when a duplicate triplet is detected across sources, rather than discarding
the duplicate. This brought Multi-DB from 0% → 2.6% (via UniProt cross-referencing
PSP entries).

All prompts updated: PSP is now Step 1, SIGNOR Step 2, UniProt Step 3.

## Key Findings

**Finding 10 — PSP unlocks the ceiling.**
Adding PSP as Step 1 pushed F1 from 0.572 → 0.861–0.869 and recall from 0.463 →
0.952. 417/433 kinases found (was 377). Only 16 kinases remain missing from the
gold standard. Peptide accuracy jumped from 0.196–0.219 → 0.976–0.980 — PSP
peptides are much higher quality than SIGNOR's.

**Finding 10b — UniProt is marginal after PSP.**
paper_informed ran 68 UniProt kinase queries on top of PSP+SIGNOR, adding 471
entries but slightly lowering F1 (0.869 → 0.861) due to precision drop (more FPs).
PSP+SIGNOR is the sweet spot by F1.

**Finding 11 — PSP+SIGNOR overlap is 0% Multi-DB.**
Despite the merge fix, PSP and SIGNOR alone yield 0% Multi-DB — they appear to
use different gene symbol or site notation conventions for overlapping relationships,
so (kinase, substrate, site) keys don't match between them. The 2.6% Multi-DB in
paper_informed came from UniProt cross-referencing PSP entries. Worth investigating
for the paper — which kinases have notation disagreements between PSP and SIGNOR?

**Finding 11b — Together AI instability.**
Two server errors today (503 then 500) cut pipeline_informed short after PSP+SIGNOR.
The fallback accumulator handled this gracefully — no data was lost.

**Behavioral note — pipeline_informed no longer loops.**
Previous run (03/31/2026) looped for 222 calls at $10.41. With PSP now providing
~15k entries in tool 1, the model has far less marginal gain from UniProt queries.
Cost dropped from $10.41 → ~$0.01 (cut short) vs ~$0.41 for paper_informed full run.

**Naive note.**
`results/naive/atlas.json` contained 379 unexpected entries — traced to a run where
local database files were present on disk (contaminated environment). The true naive
finding (Finding 1, 03/25/2026) is an empty atlas: Qwen produces nothing without data
access. Empty atlas submitted for naive condition.
